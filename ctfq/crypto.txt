CHALLENGE: The Mystery of the Sphinx's Offering
================================================================

--- HINTS ---

HINT 1: 
You are dealing with an "Encryption Oracle." This means you can feed the program whatever text you want and see how it encrypts it. Try giving the Sphinx a very simple string, like a bunch of "A"s (e.g., AAAAAAAA), and see what comes out.

HINT 2: 
If you encrypt "AAAAAAAA", the program spits out "ZEUSZEUS". Because "A" represents a shift of zero in cryptography, the Oracle is accidentally revealing its secret repeating key! This is a Vigenère Cipher, and the key is "ZEUS".

HINT 3: 
Now that you have the ciphertext (NPSEOMUFMILZV) and the secret key (ZEUS), head over to a site like CyberChef or dcode.fr and use a Vigenère Decoder to find the plaintext offering.
